# Common AI-Style Patterns in Chinese Prose

Use this checklist while editing Chinese text. The Chinese phrases are diagnostic examples and replacement examples; keep the final writing natural to its actual context.

## Formulaic Sentence Structures

| Pattern to inspect | Why it often fails | Revision direction |
|---|---|---|
| `不是 X，而是 Y` | Forced contrast or inflated claim | State Y directly, or separate the ideas. |
| `不仅仅是 X，更是 Y` | Automatic escalation | Remove the template and state the facts. |
| Grand opening with `当……时` | Begins with a generic era or trend | Start with a specific situation. |
| Repeated negative parallelism | Several `不是……` clauses in a row | Compress into one clear point. |

Examples:

- Before: `这不仅仅是效率的提升，更是工作方式的革命`
- After: `以前要三天，现在三十分钟。区别在这。`
- Before: `当数字化浪潮席卷各行各业，传统企业面临着前所未有的挑战`
- After: `做了二十年外贸的张总，去年第一次用上了AI客服。`

## Empty Judgments

Inspect phrases such as `这很关键`, `这值得深思`, `归根结底`, `说到底`, `从本质上讲`, and `本质上是`. They often tell readers what to think without adding evidence. Delete them, or replace them with a natural transition when needed.

- Before: `这很关键——数据质量决定了模型上限。`
- After: `数据质量决定了模型上限。`
- Before: `归根结底，用户要的是好用。`
- After: `用户要的就是好用。`

## Inflated Tone

| Pattern | Revision direction |
|---|---|
| Opening with `我们正处在一个……` | Remove `我们` or name a concrete subject. |
| Era framing such as `在XX时代背景下` | Start with the relevant event or fact. |
| Philosophical ending such as `这或许正是……的意义所在` | End with a concrete result or action. |
| `无疑`, `毋庸置疑`, `不可否认` | Let the evidence carry the claim. |

- Before: `我们正处在一个技术飞速迭代的时代。`
- After: Omit it and start with the next substantive sentence.
- Before: `这或许正是数字化转型的真正意义所在。`
- After: `公司今年在数字化上投了270万，省了400万。`

## Stock Vocabulary

Replace these words with context-specific, natural Chinese rather than mechanically substituting synonyms.

| Stock term | Possible direction |
|---|---|
| `赋能` | `帮`, `让……可以`, `给……用上` |
| `闭环` | `流程走通`, `从头到尾搞定` |
| `赛道` | `领域`, `行业`, `方向` |
| `底层逻辑` | `根本原因`, `核心问题` |
| `重新定义` | Describe what changed. |
| `彻底颠覆` | Describe the size of the change. |
| `打法` | `方法`, `策略` |
| `抓手` | `切入点`, `突破口` |
| `对齐` | `统一`, `确认`, `沟通一致` |
| `颗粒度` | `细节`, `细致程度` |
| `痛点` | `问题`, `麻烦` |
| `落地` | `执行`, `做出来`, `上线` |
| `倒逼` | `迫使`, `催生` |
| `窗口期` | `机会`, `时机` |
| `全链路` | `从头到尾`, `全流程` |

- Before: `通过AI赋能全链路，重塑行业底层逻辑`
- After: `用AI把整个流程跑了一遍，发现中间三个环节可以省掉。`

## Rhetorical Templates

| Pattern | Example | Revision direction |
|---|---|---|
| Generic metaphor | `犹如一匹黑马`, `像雨后春笋` | Give the concrete fact. |
| Oversized analogy | `就像当年互联网改变零售业一样` | Explain the actual comparison or remove it. |
| Balanced slogan | `X 越…，Y 越…` | Break it into factual statements. |
| Universal aphorism | `在不确定性中寻找确定性`, `长期主义` | State what someone did or decided. |

- Before: `技术越成熟，应用越广泛，挑战也越严峻。`
- After: `技术现在成熟了，用的人多了，但问题也跟着来了：去年出了三次数据泄露。`
- Before: `在不确定性中寻找确定性。`
- After: `老板的原话是：不管怎样，先把现金流保住。`
